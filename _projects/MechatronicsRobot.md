[Here is our Final Report for the Mechatronics Robot Competition]({{ "/assets/HW2.pdf" | relative_url }}) in PDF format.


For this competition, the goal was to gather more blocks than the other robot in tournament style competition. On the competition field consisting of a blue side and a yellow side surrounded by a black border, the robots would have to autonomously collect as many blocks as possible at the same time, avoiding one another and staying on the competition field. In this report, we outlined our design process, game strategy, circuitry and arduino code used for the competition. 

Individually, I worked on the arduino code, programming the motor functions and color sensor. I also assisted with building and wiring the robot. I created the circuit diagram in LTspice and the flow chart for the code in Powerpoint.
