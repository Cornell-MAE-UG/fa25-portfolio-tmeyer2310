During my time at Ashlawn Energy (summer 2025), I worked on designing and prototyping scaled down versions of redox flow batteries. Specifically, I worked on design and laser cutting silicone gaskets to prevent to leaks in the cell. This needed to be very precise as the acidic electrolyte would damage the membrane and other parts of the prototype. At first, I tried various o-rings and gaskets of varying thickness before settling on a final design which successful did not leak for weeks while we collected data and pumped acidic electrolyte through the assembly.
